[h2]Key features[/h2]
[img]https://i.imgur.com/jdunrUl.png[/img]
[b][list][*] You can change the highlight color for each button individually.

[*] Change the highlight rendering modes to negative.

[*] Adjust the size and transparency, apply pixelation and edge smoothing. You can also apply a smooth fade.

[*] It works on top of everything, even in the main menu.

[*] Turn it off when you need to, or switch rendering modes with a single key!

[*] The mod also fully supports any mods for custom cursors and [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3119712528]High FPS Support[/url][/list][/b]
